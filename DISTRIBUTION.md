## Engine and costs

Unreal Engine is distributed only as the runtime incorporated into the game. Engine source/editor tools are not included. Required engine trademarks and notices appear in CREDITS.md and the player licence. Other bundled runtime components retain their supplied notices.

There are no real-money purchases or subscriptions in this preview. A game with no directly attributable revenue does not incur Unreal Engine royalties under the current royalty provisions. Monetisation would require a fresh review of release notification, revenue exclusions and royalty obligations: https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/unreal . This document records the project's distribution approach; it does not replace any contributor's applicable licence.
